Play

Picking a file here loads it at once (ACTION 6); on the panel P5 highlights and B3-hold loads.

Kit — the 24 Voice-map keys —

record: —

Click a key to see its material below. An empty key plays its span of the break; a recorded key plays its take. ● arms a take into that key (the next gate on J6 / the panel gesture starts it); ✕ clears a recording. Class colours: kick, snare, hat, other.

Looper

—events on the bar

Loop-record writes performance events onto the bar; the count comes from the state (byte 176). Undo and Clear take the same path as the panel gestures.

Set — the break, its recorded keys, the options and the loop, on the card —

A set is sets/<name>.wav (PCM16, both planes when the material is stereo, the slice starts as cue points) beside sets/<name>.json. Sets also appear in the Sample list as “name [set]” and load from P5 + B3 like any file; a set saved just now joins that list at the next connect. Saving refuses while a take runs; the panel flashes the buttons red when the card is missing or full.

Options —

Labels come from the module's descriptor; a change is written live (GET_LIVE → BLOB to live) and is not saved until a preset is saved from Settings or /program.

Connect to read the options.
