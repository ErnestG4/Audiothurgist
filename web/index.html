Kit — the 24 Voice-map keys —

record: —

Click a key to see its material below. An empty key plays its span of the break; a recorded key plays its take. ● arms a take into that key (the next gate on J6 / the panel gesture starts it); ✕ clears a recording. Class colours: kick, snare, hat, other.

Keys: tap one to hear it and see it below · drag a key onto another to swap (a prompt when the target is taken; red when a recording would lose its only key) · long-press a key, then drop or tap another, to copy it there (many keys may play one entry) · drag a slice up from the waveform onto a key to assign it. ● and ✕ as before.

Waveform: tap inside a marker to split it there (snapped to an onset within 12 ms); tap a grid slice to select it (the grid never splits) · drag a lollipop below the wave to move a grid boundary (linked, ≥ 64 samples each side) or a marker's start — the selected marker has a square end handle too; it plays from the new point as you go and the whole slice once you let go · a second finger or Shift turns the magnets off · double-tap a marker's handle to merge it with the one ending where it starts · long-press a handle or a slice for delete / copy / promote · hold a marker's end handle still to loop its tail · the strip at the bottom pans, zooms (drag up/down, pinch, wheel) and fits the selection on a double-tap.

    Looper

    —events on the bar

    Loop-record writes performance events onto the bar; the count comes from the state (byte 176). Undo and Clear take the same path as the panel gestures.

    Set — the break, its recorded keys, the options and the loop, on the card —

    A set is sets/<name>.wav (PCM16, both planes when the material is stereo, the slice starts as cue points) beside sets/<name>.json. Sets also appear in the Sample list as “name [set]” and load from P5 + B3 like any file; a set saved just now joins that list at the next connect. Saving refuses while a take runs; the panel flashes the buttons red when the card is missing or full.

    Scope ——clip

    audio
    cv
    preset

    Ten channels on one sample clock from the firmware's rings: out L/R and in L/R (J1/J2, AC-coupled) at 48 kHz, and the six jacks J3–J8 DC-coupled at ±5 V, 2 kHz — LFOs, envelopes, sequences and gates, in or out (an output jack reads back what it drives). One snapshot per frame, so every trace starts at the same instant; each tier keeps its own window length, drawn over the shared time axis. Polled as fast as the link allows while this tab shows (≤ 30 frames/s, the state poll eased to 4 Hz). Click a channel to show or hide it — J1/J2 show themselves while a signal is present. A jack's label is its role under the current jack map, with its present voltage. Needs firmware with scope2 (2 for the jacks); older firmware shows its mono output trace here.

    Options —

    Labels come from the module's descriptor; a change is written live (GET_LIVE → BLOB to live) and is not saved until a preset is saved from Settings or /program.
