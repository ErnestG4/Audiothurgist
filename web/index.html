Kit — the 24 keys —

record —

Click a key to see its material below (on a phone, ● and ✕ act on the selected key). An empty key plays its span of the break; a recorded key plays its take. ● arms a take into that key (the next gate on J6 / the panel gesture starts it); ✕ clears a recording.

Keys: tap one to hear it and see it below · drag a key onto another to swap (a prompt when the target is taken; red when a recording would lose its only key) · long-press a key, then drop or tap another, to copy it there · drag a slice up from the waveform onto a key to assign it.

    Play's loop-record writes what you play onto the bar; the count comes from the state (byte 176). Undo and Clear take the same path as the panel's B2 / B3 in Play.

    Set — the break, its recorded keys, the options and the loop, on the card —

    A set is Audiothurgist/sets/<name>.wav (PCM16, both planes when the material is stereo, the slice starts as cue points) beside <name>.json. Sets also appear in the Sample list as “name [set]” and load from P5 + B3 like any file. Saving refuses while a take runs.

    Card — add breaks —

    Drop audio files here WAV (16/24-bit or float), AIFF, MP3, FLAC, OGG — anything this browser can open. Each becomes Audiothurgist/breaks/<name>.wav: 48 kHz, PCM16, stereo kept, the first 17 s.

      The browser decodes and converts the file; the module receives it whole and then writes it to the card itself (P5's ring fills meanwhile) — the break keeps playing and the clock keeps time throughout. One file at a time; refused while a set saves or loads, a take runs or a file loads. The module only reads its Audiothurgist folder.

      Scope ——clip

      audio
      cv
      preset

      Ten channels on one sample clock from the firmware's rings: out L/R and in L/R (J1/J2, AC-coupled) at 48 kHz, and the six jacks J3–J8 DC-coupled at ±5 V, 2 kHz — in or out (an output jack reads back what it drives). One snapshot per frame, so every trace starts at the same instant. Click a channel to show or hide it — J1/J2 show themselves while a signal is present. A jack's label is its role under the current jack map, with its present voltage. Needs firmware with scope2 (2 for the jacks); older firmware shows its mono output trace here.
