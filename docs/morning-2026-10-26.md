## What's new

- **It powers up as a sampler** (Pads, the transport stopped): the keys play
  samples, nothing else does. Options → Start-up → *As saved* brings back the
  old boot. The setting is a file on the card (`Audiothurgist/startup.json`),
  not a preset byte, so your saved presets load as before.
- **▶ / B1 held in Pads brings the beat back**: the boot preset's grammar (or
  the one you left Pads from) and starts it on the beat. With no computer
  this is the only way from the sampler start to a beat without a preset
  load. It's a call; see below.
- **Each key has its own settings** (Kit → select a key → the row under the
  keys):
  - gain −24…+6 dB;
  - tune ±24 st with cents;
  - Plays: Follow · One-shot · Held · Loop · Ratchet. Ratchet retriggers
    every whole / ½ / ¼ / ⅛ of the sample, or every 8th / 16th / 32nd at the
    tempo.

  Each edit (a whole slider drag) is one undo step, and the settings are
  saved in the set. They ride every call through the key (CV, pads, Play's
  loop), never the pattern's steps.
- **Normalise drops**: a page toggle, off by default.
- **Page fix**: the edit history no longer logs your own undo as "changed on
  the module" (it was the old selftest flake).

## Calls (the defaults are what's built)

- ▶ / B1 in Pads brings back the last beat. Keep it, or should ▶ in Pads
  stay silent?
- The sampler line offers Classic and Grids only.
- Key settings stay with the key on swap / copy (they don't move with the
  sound), and new material resets them all.
- Ratchet with no gate (Play's loop replaying a one-shot) fills one 16th
  (at least 2 hits); a held ratchet with no release stops after 8 bars.
- A looper replay uses the key's settings as they are now.
- Gain range −24…+6 dB in ½ dB steps; tune ±24 st.
- Loop played reversed falls back to plain Held.
- Normalise peaks to −1 dBFS, one gain for both channels.
